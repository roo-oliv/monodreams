## KEY FACTS a fresh session needs
- **Virtual resolution = 1280×720** (`MonoDreams.Demos/Game1.cs`). Dialogue box is on the **UI**
  target, full width. The 760×440 green field is only the **world** boundary (Main target, camera-
  centred at screen centre; world(0,0)→(640,360)). UI/HUD composite OVER Main.
- **Color fact:** `dialog box medium` + the beige panels are all `(232,207,166)`; the ONLY distinct
  warm tone is the tan panel `(220,185,138)`. So the "lighter cream inner on beige bg" look needs
  tan OUTER + cream INNER (hence `boxNinePatch`). Contrast is subtle — the panels' brown borders do
  the delineation.
- **Cow** (`Free Cow Sprites` 96×64, 32px frames) faces RIGHT; head is in the lower-right of frame
  (0,0). **Indicator** = `SproutLands/Dialogue/indicator` crop `(96,0,16,16)`.
- Nine-patch is built at runtime by `SpritePrepSystem.CreateNinePatchTexture` (cached). On UI,
  SpritePrep/MeshPrep REQUIRE `VisibleComponent` to refill the drawable.
- Yarn is compiled in-memory (`CompileYarn` in the demo); the conversation is the `YarnSource` const.
  `<<emote who kind>>`/`<<react who mark>>` arrive as `DialogueCommandMessage`; an inline command is
  flowed past via a deferred `_pendingContinue` on the next Update.
